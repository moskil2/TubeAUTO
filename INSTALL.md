# TubeAUTO - step-by-step installation

You need two apps: **Shizuku** and **TubeAUTO**. No computer needed.
On the screenshots, the red frame shows what to tap. The screenshots come from a phone set to
Polish; on your phone the same buttons are in the same place, in your language.

## Part 1. Shizuku (only once)

First install **[Shizuku from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=moe.shizuku.privileged.api)**.

Do steps 5-13 within 3 minutes. If "Shizuku isn't responding" appears or the Shizuku
notification disappears, start again from step 5.

| Step | Screen |
|---|---|
| **1.** Open the phone **Settings** and tap **About phone**. | <img src="screenshots/instrukcja/01.jpg" width="270" alt="Step 1" /> |
| **2.** Tap **Software information**. | <img src="screenshots/instrukcja/02.jpg" width="270" alt="Step 2" /> |
| **3.** Tap **Build number** **7 times**. The phone may ask for your screen lock code. | <img src="screenshots/instrukcja/03.jpg" width="270" alt="Step 3" /> |
| **4.** Done: **Developer options** now appear at the bottom of **Settings**. If they were there already, you can skip steps 1-3. | <img src="screenshots/instrukcja/04.jpg" width="270" alt="Step 4" /> |
| **5.** Open **Shizuku** and tap **Pairing**. | <img src="screenshots/instrukcja/05.jpg" width="270" alt="Step 5" /> |
| **6.** If you see this screen, tap **Notification settings**. If not, go to step 9. | <img src="screenshots/instrukcja/06.jpg" width="270" alt="Step 6" /> |
| **7.** Turn on **Allow notifications**. | <img src="screenshots/instrukcja/07.jpg" width="270" alt="Step 7" /> |
| **8.** Go back with the arrow. | <img src="screenshots/instrukcja/08.jpg" width="270" alt="Step 8" /> |
| **9.** Tap **Developer options**. | <img src="screenshots/instrukcja/09.jpg" width="270" alt="Step 9" /> |
| **10.** Tap the text **Wireless debugging** (not the switch next to it). | <img src="screenshots/instrukcja/10.jpg" width="270" alt="Step 10" /> |
| **11.** If the top says **Off**, turn the switch on and tap **Allow**. Then tap **Pair device with pairing code**. | <img src="screenshots/instrukcja/11.jpg" width="270" alt="Step 11" /> |
| **12.** A 6-digit code appears. Remember it and **do not close** this window. | <img src="screenshots/instrukcja/12.jpg" width="270" alt="Step 12" /> |
| **13.** A Shizuku notification **Pairing service found** appears at the top of the screen (if it is gone, swipe down from the very top of the screen). Tap **Enter pairing code** in it, type the code from step 12 and tap **Send**. | <img src="screenshots/instrukcja/13.jpg" width="270" alt="Step 13" /> |
| **14.** Go back to **Shizuku** and tap **Start**. | <img src="screenshots/instrukcja/14.jpg" width="270" alt="Step 14" /> |
| **15.** Shizuku starts. The window closes by itself after 3 seconds. Done: Shizuku is running. | <img src="screenshots/instrukcja/15.jpg" width="270" alt="Step 15" /> |

## Part 2. TubeAUTO

| Step | Screen |
|---|---|
| **16.** Download the **[latest TubeAUTO](https://github.com/moskil2/TubeAUTO/releases/latest)** (the `.apk` file), open it and tap **Install**. | <img src="screenshots/instrukcja/16.jpg" width="270" alt="Step 16" /> |
| **17.** Open **TubeAUTO** and tap **Enable in Android Auto**. | <img src="screenshots/instrukcja/17.jpg" width="270" alt="Step 17" /> |
| **18.** Tap **Allow all the time**. | <img src="screenshots/instrukcja/18.jpg" width="270" alt="Step 18" /> |
| **19.** Turn on **Allow permission**. | <img src="screenshots/instrukcja/19.jpg" width="270" alt="Step 19" /> |
| **20.** Go back with the arrow. | <img src="screenshots/instrukcja/20.jpg" width="270" alt="Step 20" /> |
| **21.** Tap **Enable in Android Auto** again. | <img src="screenshots/instrukcja/21.jpg" width="270" alt="Step 21" /> |
| **22.** Tap **Update**. | <img src="screenshots/instrukcja/22.jpg" width="270" alt="Step 22" /> |
| **23.** Wait until the installation finishes. If TubeAUTO closes, open it again. | <img src="screenshots/instrukcja/23.jpg" width="270" alt="Step 23" /> |
| **24.** Done: the bottom shows **Ready**. | <img src="screenshots/instrukcja/24.jpg" width="270" alt="Step 24" /> |

## Part 3. Car

**25.** Connect the phone to the car and open **TubeAUTO** on the car screen.

## Later

- **After a phone restart** Shizuku is off. You do not need it for watching in the car.
  You only need it when you update TubeAUTO: then open Shizuku and tap **Start**
  (no need to pair again).
- **After every TubeAUTO update** do steps 17-24 again.
